longBio: >-
  Rollin Brummette is an Americana singer-songwriter and guitarist who grew up
  on a 70-acre hobby farm in Grand Ledge, Michigan. His breakthrough came in
  July 2021 when he opened for ZZ Top, John Fogerty, and George Thorogood at
  Soaring Eagle Casino & Resort. That performance inspired him to pursue music
  full-time.


  By 2023, he had released three singles and relocated to Nashville, where he recorded his debut EP 'Poison Ivy' at Troubadour Recording Studio in Lansing. The six-track collection explores themes of change, growth, and relationships with a sound reminiscent of Tyler Childers and John Denver.
