  Rollin Brummette is an Americana singer-songwriter and guitarist raised on a 70-acre hobby farm in Grand Ledge, Michigan. Rooted in open fields and Midwestern work ethic, his music blends heartfelt storytelling with acoustic-driven grit. In July 2021, he took the stage at Soaring Eagle Casino & Resort, opening for ZZ Top, John Fogerty, and George Thorogood — a defining moment that solidified his decision to pursue music full-time.


  By 2023, Rollin had released three singles and made the move to Nashville to further develop his craft. He later recorded his debut EP, *Poison Ivy*, at Troubadour Recording Studio in Lansing. The six-track collection explores themes of change, growth, and relationships, delivered with an Americana sound that nods to artists like Tyler Childers and John Denver while remaining distinctly his own.
