Sometimes the biggest risks lead to the greatest rewards. After years of playing gigs around mid-Michigan, I made the decision to pack up and head to Nashville to pursue music full-time.

It wasn't an easy choice — leaving behind family, the farm, and everything I knew — but that performance at Soaring Eagle Casino showed me what's possible when you put yourself out there.

Nashville has been everything I hoped for and more. The songwriting community here is incredible, and I'm surrounded by people who share the same passion for music.
