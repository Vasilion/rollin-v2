It's been a long road to get here, but I'm thrilled to announce that my debut EP "Poison Ivy" is officially out now on all streaming platforms.

## The Journey

This EP has been in the works since the pandemic, when I first started compiling tracks and writing new material. The title track was written during a visit to my sister in Tennessee, and the rest of the songs came together after I settled in Nashville.

## The Recording

We recorded most of the EP during a weekend session at Troubadour Recording Studio in Lansing with head engineer Corey DeRushia. Corey plays bass, percussion, and electric guitar on the recording, and we also brought in violinist Sam Cooper for "Darlin."

## What's Next

I'm already working on new material and have some exciting shows coming up. Stay tuned for more music and updates.
